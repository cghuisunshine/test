How the test was framed

A backtest is only meaningful when its purchase timing, price series, and treatment of distributions are explicit.

Contribute

Start at $0 and invest $1,000 at each month-end for 240 months.

Adjust prices

Use split- and dividend-adjusted VGT prices, permitting fractional shares.

Exclude frictions

No commissions, taxes, inflation adjustment, or CAD/USD conversion.

Audit windows

Test every available overlapping 10- and 20-year monthly window through July 2026.

Important interpretation
The 11.8% and 6.25% figures are historical frequencies among heavily overlapping windows. They are not independent probabilities and should not be read as forecasts of future success.
Why adjusted prices matter
VGT had share splits and paid distributions during the period. Using raw closing prices would make early and late values inconsistent. Adjusted close provides a continuous total-return basis for this backtest.
What “9.92×” means
The final balance divided by total contributions is about 9.92. This is not a time-weighted return: each $1,000 contribution was invested for a different length of time.
